How a session runs

  1. Set a timer per problem — 12 minutes for an easy, 25 for a medium, 38 for a hard. That budget is what the schedule is built on.
  2. When the timer ends, stop guessing. Read the editorial, understand the idea, close it, then write the solution yourself from scratch. Reading a solution you can't reproduce counts for nothing.
  3. Mark the result. Press the box once for solved clean, twice to flag it for review. Flagged problems come back below after a week.
  4. Monday opens with 10 minutes of review — re-solve one or two problems from the review queue before starting anything new. That's why Monday carries a lighter load.
  5. On Wednesday, watch at 1.5× and keep a notebook. Sessions are sized at 45 minutes of playback plus 10 minutes of writing. When a video is split across two weeks, the timestamps tell you where to resume.
  6. End every Wednesday by drawing the design from memory on a blank page — no pausing, no scrubbing back. The recall is where the learning happens.
